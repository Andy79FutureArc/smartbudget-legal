Privacy Policy for SmartBudget

Effective Date: August 25, 2026 App Name: SmartBudget Package: com.smartbudget.pro

1. Introduction

Welcome to SmartBudget ("we," "our," or "us"). SmartBudget is a personal budgeting, expense tracking, and financial insights application for Android designed to help you manage accounts, track income and expenses, plan category budgets, schedule bill reminders, monitor savings goals, and receive budgeting guidance through our assistant, Milo AI.

We respect your privacy and are committed to safeguarding your personal and financial data. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how it is stored and processed, when and why it is synchronized or transmitted, and how you can manage or delete your data.

By using SmartBudget, you consent to the data practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

SmartBudget collects only the information necessary to provide and improve its budgeting features:

A. Information You Provide Directly

  • Profile & Identity Information: When you choose to sign in using Google Sign-In, we receive your name, email address, profile picture URL, and a unique user account identifier.
  • Financial & Budgeting Records: User-entered financial information, including account nicknames, bank institution labels, account types, balances, credit limits, optional masked last 4 digits of account numbers, transaction titles, amounts, dates, categories, notes, recurring flags, budgets, savings goals, contributions, bills, and payment records.
  • User Preferences: Preferred currency code and symbol, locale, budget cycle start day, and notification settings.
  • Milo AI Conversations: Questions, prompts, and chat interactions you submit to Milo AI.

B. Information Collected Automatically

  • App Usage & Analytics: User interactions (such as creating a budget, logging an expense, or opening a notification), screen views, and app launch timestamps.
  • Diagnostics & Error Logs: Crash reports, stack traces, internal error breadcrumbs, device hardware model, and Android operating system version.
  • Device Integrity Tokens: Cryptographic attestation tokens to verify device and app authenticity.

3. Financial Data

SmartBudget is an informational tracker and budgeting planner.

Scope of Financial Data:

  • Account Tracking: User-defined account labels, account classifications (e.g., Bank, Savings, Cash, Credit Card, Investment), balances, and credit limits.
  • Masked Identifiers: You may optionally input the last 4 digits of an account number for your personal reference.
  • Transactions & Budgets: Categorized income, expense, and transfer records with dates and custom notes.
  • Bills & Subscriptions: Bill titles, biller/provider names, amounts, due dates, recurrence intervals, and payment logs.

Important Financial Safeguards:
SmartBudget NEVER collects, requests, or stores full bank account numbers, debit or credit card CVV/CVC codes, card expiration dates, PINs, net banking passwords, or one-time passwords (OTPs). SmartBudget does not connect to live banking transfer networks and cannot withdraw, transfer, or move funds on your behalf.

4. Authentication and Account Data

SmartBudget offers secure authentication via Google Sign-In integrated with Supabase Auth:

  • Passwordless Authentication: Sign-in is handled securely via Android's native Credential Manager using Google ID tokens. SmartBudget never receives or stores your Google password.
  • Guest & Unauthenticated Usage: You may use SmartBudget locally without creating an account. In this mode, all data remains strictly on your local device under an isolated guest profile. When you subsequently choose to sign in, your local records can be linked to your authenticated account.

5. Local Storage and Cloud Sync

SmartBudget utilizes a hybrid storage model:

A. Local Storage (On-Device Database)

  • All your accounts, transactions, budgets, bills, savings goals, and Milo chat records are stored locally in an SQLite database on your device using Android Room (smart_budget_database).
  • This enables full offline functionality and fast data retrieval.
  • On-device security settings, such as the optional Biometric Lock, remain strictly on your device.

B. Cloud Synchronization (Supabase)

  • When you are signed in, your financial data synchronizes to a secure cloud database powered by Supabase.
  • Data in Supabase is partitioned by your unique account identifier (user_id) and protected by database Row-Level Security (RLS) policies. Only your authenticated account can read, write, or modify your synchronized data.

6. Milo AI / AI Processing

SmartBudget includes Milo AI, an assistant that provides budgeting summaries, answers personal finance questions, and evaluates purchase affordability.

How Milo AI Operates:

  • Local Deterministic Queries: Whenever possible, factual lookups (e.g., "What is my total grocery spend this month?" or "When is my electricity bill due?") are calculated entirely on your device using local database queries without sending data to external AI servers.
  • Cloud AI Processing (Firebase AI Logic / Google Gemini): When you ask open-ended, conversational, or subjective financial planning questions, SmartBudget transmits your prompt and a minimized context summary of relevant budgeting figures (e.g., category totals, balances, upcoming bill dates) to Firebase AI Logic powered by the Google Gemini 3.6 Flash model.
  • Data Minimization: Milo AI is never provided with passwords, PINs, OTPs, authentication tokens, or government identifiers.

Disclaimer:
Milo AI and SmartBudget provide informational and educational insights only. Milo AI does not provide certified financial, investment, tax, legal, credit, or loan advice. You remain solely responsible for your financial decisions.

7. Analytics and Crash Reporting

We use Google Firebase services to maintain app reliability and understand feature usage:

  • Firebase Analytics: Collects high-level, aggregated events such as screen views, app opens, and feature activations. Analytics events do not include exact transaction amounts, account numbers, or personal notes.
  • Firebase Crashlytics: Captures crash logs, stack traces, device hardware models, and OS versions when an unexpected error occurs. An anonymized user ID may be attached to crash reports to resolve account-specific technical issues.

8. App Check / Security

SmartBudget uses Firebase App Check with Google Play Integrity in production builds:

  • This service attests that incoming API requests originate from an authentic, unmodified SmartBudget application installed on a genuine Android device.
  • App Check transmits only device attestation data and does not collect or process your financial records.

9. Notifications

SmartBudget provides scheduled reminders for upcoming bills and recurring payments:

  • Local Scheduling: Reminders are scheduled on-device using Android's AlarmManager and displayed via the NotificationManager.
  • Permissions Used:
    • android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Required on Android 13 (API level 33) and above to display notifications.
    • android.permission.SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM: Used to trigger bill reminder notifications at the exact scheduled date and time.
    • android.permission.RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED: Restores your scheduled bill reminders automatically if your device restarts.
  • No Remote Marketing: We do not send unsolicited remote marketing push notifications. You can disable notifications at any time in the app settings or Android system settings.

10. How We Use Information

We process your data solely for the following purposes:

  • Providing core budgeting, expense tracking, and account management features.
  • Synchronizing your financial records across your signed-in devices.
  • Delivering local bill due-date reminders.
  • Generating customized budgeting suggestions and answers via Milo AI.
  • Diagnosing errors, monitoring system health, and fixing crashes.
  • Preventing fraudulent API usage and protecting backend infrastructure.

14. Account and Data Deletion

We provide mechanisms for you to delete your data:

  • Selecting Delete Account & Data within the app settings clears your local database cache, resets local preferences, and signs you out of your session.

B. Complete Cloud Account and Data Deletion

  • To permanently delete your Supabase authentication account and all associated cloud-synced financial records from our servers, submit an account deletion request by emailing [INSERT_YOUR_SUPPORT_EMAIL] from the email address linked to your SmartBudget account.
  • Upon verification, your account profile and all corresponding cloud database records will be permanently removed from our production servers within 30 days.

  • You can enable the in-app Biometric Lock feature to require biometric authentication (fingerprint or face unlock) before accessing the app on your device.

Please note that no method of electronic transmission or mobile storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
